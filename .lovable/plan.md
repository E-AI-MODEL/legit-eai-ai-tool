# Update all tools and SAGA prompts to the new EAI standard

## What I found
- SAGA has **27** presets (not 30). I scored all of them with the new EAI rules:
  - Only 1 of 27 is "Certified" (Litigation case memo). The other 26 are "Conditional".
  - None of the 26 asks for **counterarguments** (phase 3).
  - 17 of 27 leave **70% or more of the thinking to the AI**, so they count as AI-heavy.
- Prompt Checker and Prompt Generator already use the new rules, but they show the old long result lists. They don't have the live cockpit that SAGA has.
- Task Density Audit still uses its own older calculation and doesn't match the bar shown in SAGA.

## Step 1: Upgrade all 27 SAGA presets
- Add a "Counterarguments" section to each one: the strongest opposing view, what the other party will say, and what weakens the conclusion.
- Add lawyer work to the AI-heavy presets, for example "I mark which facts I accept" or "I choose the position before you draft", until every preset is below 70%.
- Use the same 4 phases in every preset: Facts, Analysis & legal basis, Counterarguments, Sign-off by the lawyer.
- Keep each preset's content (NL law, clauses, output format) and make no prompts shorter.
- Goal: all 27 presets "Certified", checked automatically.

## Step 2: Prompt Checker gets the SAGA treatment
- The live cockpit appears next to the prompt (work split, phases, lawyer checkpoints).
- One button: "Improve & Copy" gives the professional prompt plus a certificate.
- Remove the duplicate result lists and the "Strict Mode" switch, because the rules are now always strict.

## Step 3: Prompt Generator, simpler
- Fewer fields: task, area of law, output format, and an optional "what the lawyer decides".
- The result always passes the cockpit. The generator shows the score and certificate, then Copy.

## Step 4: Task Density Audit on the same calculation
- Use the same calculation as the SAGA bar, so the numbers are the same everywhere.
- Paste a prompt or describe the steps, see who does what, and get advice on which step to take back.

## Step 5: Check
- Automatic test: all 27 presets "Certified" and below 70% AI work.
- Phone-size check of all four tools.

## Technical notes
- The presets in `src/data/sagaPresets.ts` are rewritten by hand, not generated by a script, so each one stays specific to its area of law.
- All tools use `assessPrompt` / `humanCheckpoints` from `src/lib/eai/`. Extract the cockpit from `SagaPromptChecker` into a shared `EaiCockpit` component.
- New test in `eai.test.ts`: for each preset, certificate verdict = Certified and density < 0.7.
- Still no backend or AI. The chat window stays a separate decision.
